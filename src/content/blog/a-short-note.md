Short posts work too. With no headings there is no "On this page" list, and the text simply runs down the middle of
the page.

A useful habit when instrumenting a service: decide what question each signal answers before adding it. A trace
answers _where did the time go_, a metric answers _how often and how much_, and a log answers _what exactly happened
here_. If a new signal does not answer a question someone will actually ask, it is cost without benefit.

That is the whole note.
